Lustre is a local-first detailing assistant. You can use it without an account. If you choose Sign in with Google, Lustre can save your job file, kit, paint readings, and photos to a hidden app-data folder in your own Google Drive so you can restore them on another device. Lustre does not run its own account database.

Who we are

Lustre is operated by Caleb Wang. For privacy questions or requests, contact lustreaisupport@gmail.com.

Information Lustre handles

Google account information

If you sign in with Google, Lustre receives your basic Google profile information: name, email address, and profile identifier. Lustre uses this to show which account is connected and to keep the backup associated with that account. Lustre requests the drive.appdata scope, which lets it create and read only the files it creates in the hidden app-data folder. It cannot browse, read, or change the rest of your Google Drive.

Job and app content

Lustre stores the content you enter, including vehicle details, client first names, notes, service selections, checklist progress, timers, kit and chemical entries, paint-depth readings, settings, and inspection photos. This content is stored on your device and, if you sign in, in your Google Drive app-data folder.

AI chat, photos, and voice

When you use chat, photo review, or Gemini voice features, the text, images, video frames, and prompts needed for that request are sent through our hosting provider to the Google Gemini API so it can generate a response. If you enable Cloud listening, short audio clips containing speech are sent to the speech-to-text service to detect the wake phrase and command. Do not submit sensitive personal, financial, health, or confidential information that you do not want processed by those services.

Location and weather

If you request site weather, your browser may ask for your approximate location. The coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo to retrieve the forecast. Lustre caches the resulting weather on your device so the last forecast can still be shown offline.

Notifications

If you enable headphone alerts or push notifications, your browser or push service creates a subscription that is used to deliver timer and job alerts. The subscription may identify your browser to the push service.

No advertising or sale

Lustre does not sell your personal information, use it for advertising, or include third-party advertising trackers.

How we use information

  • Provide the job file, checklist, timers, paint map, kit, photos, client texts, and app settings.
  • Process AI chat, image review, voice output, and wake-word commands you request.
  • Back up and restore your data when you choose Google sign-in.
  • Show weather at your location when you request it.
  • Deliver alerts and keep the app working offline.
  • Protect the service, diagnose failures, and comply with legal obligations.

How information is stored and shared

On your device: Job data, photos, paint readings, and settings are stored in browser localStorage and IndexedDB. Clearing site data or uninstalling the app can delete them.

In your Google Drive: If you sign in, Lustre creates lustre-job.json and lustre-photos.json in your Google Drive app-data folder. These files are controlled by your Google account and are subject to Google's terms and privacy policy.

Service providers: Lustre uses EdgeOne Pages for hosting and serverless functions, Google Gemini for AI and speech processing, Google Identity and Drive for optional sign-in and backup, Open-Meteo for weather, and browser or push services for notifications. These providers process information to deliver their part of the service.

Google API Limited Use: Lustre's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

  • Local data: Remains on your device until you clear the app's site data or delete it through your browser or operating system.
  • Google Drive backup: Use Settings > Google account > Delete cloud backup while signed in, delete the files through Google Drive, or remove Lustre's access at Google Account permissions. Signing out stops future backups but does not delete existing files.
  • AI and hosting requests: Processed to return a response and handled under the relevant provider's retention practices. Do not send information you need to keep strictly confidential.
  • Push subscriptions: Remain until they expire, are replaced, or are removed by your browser or push service.

Security

Lustre is served over HTTPS. Google access tokens are kept in memory and are not written to local storage. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and rights

You can use Lustre without signing in, decline location or notification permissions, sign out at any time, and delete local or cloud data as described above.
